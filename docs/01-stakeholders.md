# Stakeholders and responsibilities

This is the proposed stakeholder model for the fictional case study.
The HR department is assumed to be the business owner of the employee portal.

| Stakeholder | Main concern | Contribution to the project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IT leadership / project sponsor | Business value, budget, priorities, and timeline | Approves objectives, budget, and major scope changes. |
| HR department / business owner | Employee needs and business processes | Defines required portal functions and confirms business acceptance after migration. |
| Application teams | Suitable development environments and application dependencies | Provide technical requirements, identify integrations, and validate application behaviour. |
| Operations team | Reliable services and clear support responsibilities | Defines monitoring, recovery, support, and operational handover requirements. |
| Security and compliance team | Data protection, access control, and applicable obligations | Defines control requirements with the relevant specialists and reviews evidence that they are met. |
| Finance and controlling | Project costs, ongoing spending, and cost allocation | Provides financial requirements and agrees how application and shared-service costs are reported. |
| Procurement and contract management | Contract terms, service continuity, and transition obligations | Clarifies contractual deadlines, responsibilities, and provider handover obligations. |
| Existing infrastructure provider | Current services and an agreed transition | Provides information about the current environment and supports the agreed transition activities. |

## Role of the Solution Architect

The Solution Architect brings these stakeholders together, clarifies their
requirements, explains technical options and trade-offs, and maintains a
consistent design from the business objectives through to operational handover.
